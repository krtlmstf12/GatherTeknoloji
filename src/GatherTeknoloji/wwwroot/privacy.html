Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Gather Technology collects, uses, and protects information when you visit gatherteknoloji.com or use our products — including ChargeRoute and Gather News.

1. Overview

Gather Technology (“Gather”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) operates this website and digital products available on the web and in app stores. This Privacy Policy describes the information we may collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

By using our website or products, you acknowledge this policy. Product-specific notices or store policies may provide additional detail where indicated.

2. Information we collect

Depending on the product and the permissions you grant, we may collect:

  • Account and contact details — such as name, email address, or other information you provide when you create an account or contact us.
  • Device information — device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and mobile network information.
  • Usage data — how you use our apps and website, including features accessed and approximate time spent.
  • Advertising identifiers — advertising ID (such as GAID or IDFA) where used to serve personalized or contextual ads.
  • Location data — approximate or precise location when a product needs it (for example ChargeRoute charging and routing features), only when you grant permission.
  • Preferences and content you submit — such as news sources, saved routes, feedback, or settings.
  • Log and diagnostics data — crash reports, error logs, and performance data to improve stability.

3. How we use your information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide, operate, and improve our website and product features
  • Personalise experiences where you have opted in (for example news feeds or route suggestions)
  • Show relevant advertisements through advertising partners, where ads are part of a product
  • Analyse usage patterns to enhance user experience
  • Diagnose technical issues and fix bugs
  • Respond to support requests and communicate with you
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Product-specific data

ChargeRoute

May process location, route, and charging-related data to find stations and plan EV journeys. Location is used only with your permission. Product site: chargeroute.app.

Gather News

May process reading preferences, selected sources, and interaction data to personalise feeds and AI-assisted briefings. Product site: gathernews.app.

5. Advertising

Some of our apps may display ads served by third-party advertising networks (including but not limited to Google AdMob and other partners listed where we publish an app-ads.txt file). These partners may collect and use data as described in their own privacy policies to serve targeted or contextual advertisements.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by adjusting your device settings (for example Google Settings → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization, or iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).

6. Third-party services

Our products may integrate third-party SDKs and services for maps, charging data, news publishers, AI providers, analytics, crash reporting, payments, and monetization. Those providers have their own privacy policies and data practices. We encourage you to review them. Examples may include:

  • Google / AdMob privacy policy
  • Firebase / Google Analytics privacy
  • AppLovin privacy policy

Links from our website or products to external sites are provided for convenience and do not imply endorsement.

7. Data retention and deletion

We retain personal data only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, operate our products, or as required by law. You may request deletion of your data by contacting us at the email address below. Where a product offers an in-app account or data deletion flow, you may also use that path.

8. Children’s privacy

Our apps and website are not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child, please contact us so we can delete it.

9. Data security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, and in some cases to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at info@gatherteknoloji.com.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date on this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of our website or products after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.

12. Contact us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, contact us:

  • Company: Gather Technology
  • Email: info@gatherteknoloji.com
  • Website: gatherteknoloji.com
